Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, optional email address, delivery address, selected products, quantities and any notes you provide.

How we use information

We use this information to receive, review, confirm, prepare and arrange delivery of your order, and to contact you if clarification is needed. We do not use checkout details for unrelated marketing without appropriate permission.

Order emails and service providers

Checkout information is sent to BK Agro through a server-side email delivery provider. The provider processes the information to deliver the order notification. Your hosting and email providers may also process limited technical data needed to operate the service.

Browser storage and cookies

The website may store your cart, checkout preferences and cookie choice in your browser to improve your shopping experience. You can clear this information through your browser settings.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect order information. No internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We retain order information only as reasonably necessary for customer service, business records and applicable legal obligations.

Your requests

To request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable obligations, email info@bkagrobd.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@bkagrobd.com.
