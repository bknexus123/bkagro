The website cannot guarantee mail delivery until verified and tested. On failure it shows a manual email link and WhatsApp option.
This website does not process payments; it submits order requests.
For production reliability, replace the third-party form service with a secure backend or transactional email API with spam protection, consent, monitoring, and server-side validation.
