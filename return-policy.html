Return & Refund Policy

Report a problem

If fresh meat, eggs or another perishable item is incorrect, missing, damaged or appears spoiled on arrival, please contact BK Agro within 24 hours of delivery. Include your order reference, phone number and clear photos where possible.

Eligibility and verification

We review complaints about incorrect products, missing quantities, transit damage and quality issues on arrival. Please keep affected products refrigerated or appropriately stored while we investigate. Any return or replacement is subject to verification and food-safety requirements.

Change-of-mind requests

Because fresh meat and eggs are perishable, change-of-mind returns after delivery are generally not accepted. Please contact us before processing or dispatch if you wish to cancel.

Possible resolutions

Where a complaint is verified, we may offer a replacement, partial or full refund, or another agreed resolution, depending on the circumstances and product availability.

Refund processing

If a refund is approved, we will communicate the refund method and expected processing timeframe. Bank or mobile-wallet processing may take additional time.

Contact

Email info@bkagrobd.com with your order details and supporting photos.
