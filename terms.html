Terms & Conditions

Orders and confirmation

Submitting the checkout form sends an order request to BK Agro. An order is accepted only after our team confirms product availability, final quantities, delivery arrangements and payment details by phone or email.

Prices, quantities and processing

Prices displayed on the website are estimates based on the selected quantities. Meat is charged using its actual processed weight, which may differ from the estimate. Any additional preparation or delivery charges will be communicated before confirmation.

Delivery and payment

Delivery coverage, schedules, charges and available payment methods will be confirmed directly with the customer. Delays may occur due to supply, weather or operational conditions.

Changes and cancellations

Please contact us promptly if you need to change or cancel an order. Cancellation may not be possible after preparation or dispatch has started.

Product concerns

Please inspect your order on receipt. If an item is missing, incorrect, damaged or appears unsuitable, contact us within 24 hours with your order reference and photographs where possible. See our Return & Refund Policy.

Customer information

We use the details submitted through checkout to process and follow up on orders. Please see our Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about an order can be sent to info@bkagrobd.com.
